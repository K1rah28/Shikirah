ABOUT ME

A little about myself.

Shikirah Nuena is a Civil Engineering student at the University of Rizal System. While engineering is her academic discipline, creativity has always been an equally important part of her journey. She enjoys expressing ideas through painting, illustration, and visual storytelling while continuously exploring new ways to merge technical knowledge with artistic thinking.

Beyond academics, she actively participates in student organizations where she contributes through documentation, publicity, creative design, and event coordination. These experiences have strengthened her communication skills, leadership, adaptability, and collaborative mindset.

Whether creating artwork for exhibitions, designing promotional materials, or working on engineering projects, Shikirah believes every project should combine creativity, purpose, and attention to detail.
